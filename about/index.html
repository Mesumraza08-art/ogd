Our approach

POWERING HOMES.EMPOWERINGBUSINESSES.

OGD Electric works across residential, commercial, industrial and mechanical projects. That range means one team can handle the panel, the lighting, the EV charger, the gate operator and the loading dock, without handing the job between trades.
