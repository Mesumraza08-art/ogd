Do you sell gates and garage doors or only service them?

Can you add a keypad or fob to an existing gate?

Keypads, fobs and intercoms are part of our access control work. Send us a photo of your gate operator with a quote request.

Yes. Dock leveller systems and dock equipment installation and service are part of our work.
