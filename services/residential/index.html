FAQ

Questions about residential work

Do you handle panel upgrades and service changes?

Yes. Panel upgrades and service changes are a core part of our residential work. Call or send a quote request with a photo of your current panel and we will talk through what your home needs.

Can you install an EV charger in my garage?

Yes. We install EV chargers and the dedicated circuits they need. Let us know the charger model and where you would like it mounted.

Are you ESA certified and insured?

Yes. OGD Electric is licensed, insured and ESA certified.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes. We offer 24/7 emergency electrical service. Call 647-381-5984.
