While you wait

Stay safe first.

General safety guidance. If anyone is hurt, there is a fire, or you smell burning, call 911 first.

  1. Keep clear

    Stay away from sparking equipment, damaged wiring, downed lines and water near electrical equipment.

  2. Shut off if safe

    If you can reach your panel safely and without standing in water, switch off the affected breaker or the main.

  3. Call OGD Electric

    Call 647-381-5984 and describe what you see. Photos help if it is safe to take them.
